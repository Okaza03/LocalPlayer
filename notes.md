# Valo v0.8.3

Subtitles in the player, and featured titles open from their banner.

### New
- **Subtitles:** pick subtitles from the subtitle button in the player. Valo shows the ones your server finds for a movie or episode (SRT, WebVTT and ASS/SSA), and the language you pick comes on by itself next time. On the computer, C turns them on and off. Servers list them in the library's new `subtitles` field; the reference server picks them up from a Subs folder in each movie's or series' folder.
- **Click the banner:** clicking anywhere on a featured movie or series on the home page opens it, not just More info.
