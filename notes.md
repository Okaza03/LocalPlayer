# Valo v0.8.1

Valo remembers its window, and a flicker in the player is fixed.

### New
- **Remembers its window:** Valo opens the way you closed it: maximized, or at the same size and in the same place (on Wayland, the desktop picks the place). Fullscreen isn't remembered, so closing from the player opens Valo as it was before.

### Fixed
- **No more flicker in the player:** a box that sometimes flashed in the bottom-right corner (what's behind the window showing through) is gone.
