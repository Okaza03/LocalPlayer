# Valo v0.8.0

Your Discord profile shows what you're watching, the player shows the time left, and several keyboard fixes.

### New
- **Discord status:** your Discord profile shows the movie or series you're watching, the episode, and how much is left; while you browse, it says so. It's on by default; turn it off in Settings under Playback.
- **Time left:** the player shows how long you've watched on the left and how much is left on the right.
- **Volume at a glance:** pressing the up or down arrow shows the volume slider for a few seconds, so you can see the level.
- **Press Enter to sign in:** on the sign-in screen, Enter signs you in.
- **What's new after every update:** this window appears after each update, with a link to the full release notes.

### Fixed
- **Esc no longer freezes Valo:** in the player it closes an open menu or leaves fullscreen.
- **Player keys always work:** after clicking a button or the volume slider, Space and the arrow keys no longer also act on that control (Space used to undo itself).
- **Usernames in any case:** Valo treats "user" and "User" as the same user on this device. Servers decide whether sign-in ignores case; the bundled server now does.

### Changed
- The bottom bar shows the Valo version.
