# Valo v0.6.0

Pages for the people in your library and for your own profile, a cleaner Continue watching row, and more ways to sort.

### New
- **Cast and crew pages:** click a cast member, director or creator to see every movie and show they're in, with the character they play, the years they span, their genres and who they often work with.
- **Profile page:** Profile in the account menu now opens a full page with your watch time, finished movies and episodes, completed shows, recently watched titles, favorite genres and most watched shows.
- **More info on hover:** every movie and show card shows a More info button when you hover it.
- **Remove from Continue watching:** hover a card in Continue watching, click the three dots and choose Remove. Your resume point is kept, and the title comes back the next time you watch it.
- **Sort by director:** the Movies and TV Shows pages can sort by director (creator for shows), and a new button switches any sort between ascending and descending.
- **Search by director:** search finds titles by their director or creator and links to their page.

### Changed
- **Featured and You might also like are random** instead of newest or alphabetical. They stay the same until you restart the app.
- **Clear watch history is gone** from Settings.
