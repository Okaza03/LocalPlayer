# Valo v0.8.2

Make Valo look like the service you're used to, bookmark titles straight from their cards, and a few playback and TV fixes.

### New
- **App styles:** Settings > Appearance lets you pick an app style that lays Valo out like Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max, YouTube or Apple TV: the home page, cards, details pages, episode lists and the player. Each style is shown as a small diagram of its layout.
- **Color themes:** pick System, Dark, Light, Black, Dim or Midnight. The style keeps its accent color; the theme sets the rest.
- **Bookmark from the card:** hover over a movie or series on the home page, Movies or TV shows and click the bookmark in its corner to add it to My list.
- **What's new on TV:** the TV app now shows what's new after it updates.

### Fixed
- **Movies stuck at 0:00:** some files whose audio starts a moment after the video only began playing after a seek. They now play straight away.
- **TV updates install with one press:** the update installs directly, reuses a finished download, and carries on by itself after you allow Valo to install apps.
