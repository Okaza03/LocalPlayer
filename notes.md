# Valo v0.6.1

Your computer no longer goes to sleep in the middle of a movie.

### Fixed
- **No more sleep screen while watching:** Valo keeps the screen awake while a video is playing, so the computer no longer locks, blanks or sleeps mid-movie. When you pause or close the player, the normal sleep timer comes back.
